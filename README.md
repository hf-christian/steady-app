# Steady

A friendly habit tracker for quitting bad habits: streaks, milestones, rewards you set for yourself, money saved, and a kind bounce-back after a slip.

**Open it:** https://hf-christian.github.io/steady-app/

**Add it to your home screen**
- iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

**Privacy:** everything you enter stays on your phone, in the browser's storage. Nothing is sent to a server. The daily reminder is an event in your own calendar, and you can delete it at any time.

**Not medical advice:** Steady is a habit support tool, not medical treatment or medical advice. If you think you may be dependent on alcohol or another substance, talk to a doctor.

This repo holds only the built app. It's generated from a private project, so changes made here directly will be overwritten.
